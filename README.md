# Retail Customer Behaviour & Business Analytics

## Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer shopping behaviour and identifying actionable business insights. The project uses Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI to analyze customer transactions, purchasing patterns, product trends, and sales channels.

## Business Objective

The objective is to analyze consumer shopping data to identify trends, understand customer behaviour, improve customer engagement, and support data-driven marketing and product strategies.

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer purchasing patterns
- Demographic-based behaviour
- Product category trends
- Impact of discounts and reviews
- Seasonal purchasing behaviour
- Payment preferences
- Online vs. offline sales channels
- Factors influencing repeat purchases

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer transaction and behavioural information, including:

- Customer demographics
- Product categories
- Purchase information
- Discounts
- Reviews
- Payment methods
- Purchase frequency
- Sales channels
- Seasonal purchasing patterns

## Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** – Data inspection, preprocessing, and preliminary analysis
- **Python** – Data cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis, and statistical analysis
- **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation and numerical analysis
- **SQL** – Business analysis, filtering, aggregation, and querying
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and KPI visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Exploratory data analysis and analytical workflow
- **Gamma** – Project presentation and business storytelling

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading & Inspection

Loaded the customer behaviour dataset and examined its structure, data types, missing values, duplicates, and key variables.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Performed data cleaning and transformation using Excel and Python to prepare the dataset for analysis.

Key activities included:

- Handling missing values
- Removing duplicate records
- Correcting data types
- Standardizing categorical variables
- Preparing analytical features

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed EDA using Python to identify:

- Customer purchasing patterns
- Product category trends
- Demographic behaviour
- Seasonal trends
- Discount and review patterns
- Payment preferences
- Online vs. offline purchasing behaviour

### 4. SQL Business Analysis

Used SQL queries to analyze the cleaned dataset and answer key business questions through:

- Filtering
- Aggregation
- GROUP BY analysis
- Conditional analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Product and sales analysis

### 5. Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key business metrics and customer behaviour.

The dashboard covers:

- Customer KPIs
- Purchasing trends
- Product performance
- Customer demographics
- Seasonal patterns
- Sales channel comparison
- Payment preferences
- Customer behaviour insights

### 6. Business Insights & Reporting

Interpreted analytical findings and translated them into actionable insights for marketing, customer engagement, and product strategy.

### 7. Presentation

Created a professional project presentation using Gamma to communicate the methodology, findings, visualizations, and business recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

The project answers questions such as:

1. Which customer segments contribute most to purchasing activity?
2. Which product categories have the highest demand?
3. How do discounts influence customer purchasing behaviour?
4. How do purchasing patterns vary across demographics?
5. How does online purchasing compare with offline purchasing?
6. What payment methods are most preferred by customers?
7. What seasonal trends exist in customer purchases?
8. Which factors are associated with repeat purchases?
9. How do customer reviews relate to purchasing behaviour?
10. What insights can be used to improve marketing and product strategies?
